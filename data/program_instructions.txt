
-- Welcome! --
This program will teach you how to reccognize, read, and write the Deseret alphabet! It uses spaced repetition - 
this means that questions which you frequently get right will be shown to you less, and questions which give you 
trouble will appear more so you can improve.

-- How to use the program --
The flashcards mode will allow you to familiarize yourself with the individual 
characters of the alphabet and their pronunciations. 

Once you're confident with the individual characters, you can use the typing/spelling mode to practice
using a Deseret keyboard, recognize words, and spell them. 

IMPORTANT NOTE: The typing/spelling mode teaches you to recognize and spell words as they were pronounced
in 1850s Utah, not modern Standard American English! Some pronunciations of familiar words will be different.
