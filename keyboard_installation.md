## How to install a Deseret alphabet keyboard

In order to get full functionality from the program and to type the Deseret, you will need to download an application called Keyman to use custom keyboards on your computer. You can download Keyman by selecting the Deseret keyboard at this link: 
https://keyman.com/keyboards/deseret 

Click 'install keyboard' and follow the setup instructions.
Remember to download new versions of Keyman as soon as updates are released, as this will prevent issues with displaying and typing your text!

This link provides keymapping information and help on how to use Keyman's Deseret keyboard: 
https://help.keyman.com/keyboard/deseret/1.2/deseret

## Tips for using the keyboard

Due to the nature of typing the Deseret on a Latin-based keyboard, there are a few quirks that can lead to typos. I've outlined them here so you can avoid them in advance!

- You might be tempted to type 𐐳 when you mean 𐐲 - remember that the former uses the u key, and the latter the o key. Many words in English with the shwa sound (𐐲) are spelled with 'u', so watch out.
  
- Symbols which are typed with two key-presses can be tricky. For example, you might need 𐐮𐐮 or 𐐮𐐯 - which would automatically correct to 𐐨 and 𐐴. Avoid this by pressing the space key, typing the second letter, then deleting the space. Keep an eye out for these, they're easy to miss!
